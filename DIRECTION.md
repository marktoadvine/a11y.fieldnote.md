# Direction: portable accessibility fieldnotes

AFS helps designers and developers carry accessibility choices, questions, and responsibilities into a project. A record should be easy to author, understandable at handoff, and readable by people and software.

The 0.3.0 draft centers on a versioned component record and notes with stable IDs. Component identity, platform, and references preserve context. Optional configurations, benchmarks, note status, and check definitions support detail when it is useful.

Custom topics, statuses, platforms, and fields remain welcome. Preserve shared field meanings so tools can recognize them. Accessibility depends on people, language, region, platform, and implementation; document those nuances rather than treating notes as a universal checklist.

Keep the core small and make added structure purposeful. Check definitions describe procedures; verification-result records and certification remain outside the format.

Success means someone can understand the accessibility choices and open questions and use them in their project with little explanation.
